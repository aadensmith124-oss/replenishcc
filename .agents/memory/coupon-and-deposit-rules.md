---
name: Coupon and deposit rules
description: Product rules for checkout coupons and configurable deposit minimums.
---

Coupons apply only to log-product checkout paid from a member's account balance, not to deposits. Administrators set each coupon's maximum total redemptions; only successful checkouts consume one redemption.

The administrator-controlled minimum deposit applies to both manual and cryptocurrency deposits. Keep referral reward qualification as its own threshold.

**Why:** The user chose an admin-set coupon usage limit and clarified that the deposit minimum should cover both payment paths without changing referral qualification.

**How to apply:** Preserve these scopes when changing checkout discounts, deposit settings, or referral qualification.
